# 🚀 NekoSalesAI

> An AI-powered autonomous sales employee built by **Neko** that helps businesses find customers, qualify leads, automate follow-ups, prioritize opportunities, negotiate intelligently, and ultimately close more deals.

---

# Overview

NekoSalesAI is an AI Sales Operating System designed to become a complete digital sales employee.

Unlike traditional CRM software that only stores customer information, NekoSalesAI actively thinks, analyzes, makes decisions, creates tasks, tracks customer history, recommends actions, and continuously learns from every interaction.

The long-term vision is to build an autonomous AI capable of finding prospects, engaging customers, managing conversations, negotiating deals, coordinating human teams when necessary, and handling the entire customer lifecycle with minimal human intervention.
