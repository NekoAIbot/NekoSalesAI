How it works

Three things happen, in this order

You describe the job. {{ company.agent_name }} prices what it takes to build it and shows the arithmetic. You pay, it builds, you get the keys.

  1. 01

    You say what you need

    Tell {{ company.agent_name }} the job in your own words. It names the AI that does it — or says plainly that it cannot build that one yet, and gets you a person instead.

  2. 02

    It prices the build, line by line

    Every line is one thing that has to be built: the channels it answers on, the volume it carries, the systems it has to talk to. No card needed to see the number.

  3. 03

    It builds it and hands it over

    The agent goes live under your name, on your catalog, with your login emailed to you. From then on it is yours — and it is the one talking to your buyers, not {{ company.agent_name }}.
