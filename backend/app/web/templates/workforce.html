Provable restraint

Nera works from what you actually configure

Most AI products promise anything to close a sale. Nera is built the other way: it works from your configured knowledge, your verified prices, your approved policies. If something is not configured, Nera does not pretend it knows it — it says so and escalates to a human.

1 No invented prices. Every price is computed server-side from configuration. The browser never carries a price.
2 No invented policies. Off-script terms stop and wait for human approval. Nera never agrees a discount on its own.
3 No invented capabilities. If a product is not live or configurable, the page says so — it does not imply it exists.
4 Every answer traceable. The worker shows the signals it read and the rule it followed — on every reply.
What restraint looks like
C
Can you do cold email outreach? We have a list of 10,000 leads we want you to work through.
N
I do not do cold outreach. Neither Nera nor anything it builds sends unsolicited messages. I only answer people who come to you.
C
What about a discount? Our budget is ₦80,000/month.
N
I cannot agree to a discount. Pricing is computed from what the build has to do, and I am not authorized to change it. I will raise this with a human who can review your situation.
