Provable Restraint

Nera works only from what you actually configure

Generic bots promise anything to close a conversation. Nera is built with rigorous restraint: it operates strictly from your configured knowledge, verified prices, and approved policies. If an item is unknown, Nera never fabricates an answer — it says so and escalates to human review.

1 No invented prices. Every price is computed server-side from configuration. The browser never carries a price.
2 No unauthorized discounts. Off-script terms stop and wait for human approval. Nera never agrees a discount on its own.
3 No invented capabilities. If a product or feature is not live or configurable, the agent explicitly says so.
4 Every answer traceable. The agent logs the exact reasoning path and rule citation on every single response.
