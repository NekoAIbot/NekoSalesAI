How a build is priced

Five dimensions, each with a real cost

Base
Every product starts at its base price. A sales representative is not the same build as a support agent — one quotes and closes, the other answers from knowledge — so they start at different figures.
Channels
The web widget is included. Every additional channel — Telegram, WhatsApp, Email — is real integration work and carries a real add. You only pay for the channels you actually configure.
Volume
Conversation volume is priced in bands, not per message, so you can tell what you will pay before you know your traffic. Beyond 5,000 conversations/month is custom — talk to us.
Add-ons
Languages, CRM sync, calendar booking, payment collection — each is priced by what it adds. English is included. Every additional language is translation and testing work, not a config flag.
Workflow
More workflow steps mean a more custom conversation flow. Configured per build, up to 5 steps on the standard configuration.

No fixed tiers. No rounding. The quote on /build shows every line that made up the figure — and the server is the source of truth for that figure, not this page.
