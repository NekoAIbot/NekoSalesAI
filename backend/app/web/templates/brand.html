Visual identity — four directions

Four logo concepts for Nera

Each concept is a complete identity: mark, wordmark, favicon, mobile icon, monochrome and light/dark variants. They are presented as options, not a final choice — the production logo is not replaced until one is selected.

Concept A

Nera-focused abstract mark

A single organic form suggesting a voice, a signal, or a presence — Nera as the speaker, not a machine. The mark reads as something alive and restrained rather than engineered.

Concept C

Elegant typographic wordmark

Nera set as the primary asset, with a small geometric accent. The identity lives in the letterforms and the spacing, not in a separate symbol. This is the direction that says “a serious company,” not “a tech startup.”
