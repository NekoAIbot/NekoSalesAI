The principle

Provable restraint

Nera's most important feature isn't what it can do. It's what it won't make up. This isn't a marketing claim — it's how the engine is built.

When Nera doesn't know the answer, it says so — and escalates to a human who can decide.

✓ Nera does not invent prices
✓ Nera does not invent policies
✓ Nera does not invent capabilities
✓ Nera does not make promises it cannot keep
✓ Nera knows when to stop and escalate
